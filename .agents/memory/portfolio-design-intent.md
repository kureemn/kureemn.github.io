---
name: Portfolio design intent
description: User's expectations for meaningful portfolio redesigns.
---

The user considers color-and-typography-only changes insufficient for a portfolio refresh. They want more visual presentation and clearer information organization, while remaining professional and slightly artsy.

**Why:** The user said the refreshed site still felt too text-heavy and its structure had not changed much.

**How to apply:** Evaluate redesigns by how quickly visitors understand the person and their work, not just by the palette. Keep their name clear in the main hero rather than repeated in the top-left navigation, and preserve the explicit value statement “I help teams make better product decisions.”

Check whole-page scroll bounds on narrow viewports, not just whether the text appears to fit.

**Why:** A transformed decorative shape caused mobile horizontal scrolling even after the large name was correctly sized.

**How to apply:** Include narrow-phone widths in both-theme layout checks whenever a redesign introduces rotated or oversized decorative graphics.
